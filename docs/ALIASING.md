# Oscillator aliasing measurements

Generated by `gnarl_tests "[aliasing]"`. Each engine plays C7 (2093 Hz) at 48 kHz, filters off, unison 1. The figure is the energy found away from the harmonic series of the note (more than 6 FFT bins from any harmonic, 32768-point Blackman-Harris), relative to total energy. Everything there is aliasing or other non-harmonic error.

How each engine avoids aliasing: Analog, Wavetable, Grain/Formant and the Shaper rebuild a single cycle and band-limit it with an FFT for the note being played (hard sync on the Analog engine is computed inside that cycle, so it is exact). HyperSaw reads band-limited saw tables (and, when synced, a band-limited synced-saw cycle). Harmonic drops partials above Nyquist. FM runs 4x oversampled. Only the oscillator-chain Sync link (one oscillator resetting another that runs at an unrelated pitch) cannot be pre-computed into a cycle; it is corrected with a 32-tap Kaiser-windowed BLEP and some aliasing remains at high pitches (see the Chain Sync row; the link source here is a fifth below, i.e. a non-integer ratio).
